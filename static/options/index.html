Security

Reset erases all settings and today’s usage. Enter RESET, then confirm again. Local-only storage means a forgotten PIN cannot be recovered remotely.

About & privacy

The extension stores settings, a salted PIN verifier, today’s usage and overrides locally. It requests storage and access only to YouTube. A person with Chrome or developer-mode access can disable, remove, or clear the extension.
